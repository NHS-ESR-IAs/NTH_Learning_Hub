📸 How to Capture & Submit Completion Screenshots

To obtain your system access credentials, you must provide proof that you have completed each prerequisite module on ESR.

1
Reach the Completion Page

Complete all sections of the eLearning on ESR until you see the final screen displaying "Course Completed" or your name and pass score.

2
Capture the Screen (Trust PC)

Press Windows + Shift + S to open the Windows Snipping Tool, or press PrtScn (Print Screen). Select the full completion window.

3
Save or Paste into ServiceNow

Open the appropriate ServiceNow request form (TrakCare or ICE/PACS/MediViewer), paste your screenshot (Ctrl + V) directly into the attachment box, and submit your request.

Tip for Line Managers: When booking new starters onto ward rosters, ensure their ServiceNow requests are submitted at least 5 working days before their first clinical shift.
